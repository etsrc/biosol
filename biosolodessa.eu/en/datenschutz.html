Privacy Made Simple

We take the protection of your data very seriously. This website follows the principle of absolute data minimization:

  • No Cookies: We use neither technically necessary nor tracking cookies.
  • No Tracking: No analytics tools (e.g., Google Analytics) and no user tracking.

Server Log Files

Our hosting provider automatically collects standard information in server log files (browser type, operating system, time, IP address). This is technically necessary (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) to deliver the website reliably and protect against attacks. This data is not combined with other data sources.

Contact Form

When you send us an inquiry via the contact form, your data (name, email address, and your message) is stored solely for processing your specific request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). No data is shared with third parties. The data is deleted as soon as the request is resolved and no legal retention obligations apply.

Your Rights

You have the right at any time to free information, correction, restriction, or deletion of your stored personal data. Simply contact us via the contact form.

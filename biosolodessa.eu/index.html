Log Construction

Log Construction

As it grows, a tree produces oxygen and binds CO₂. Wood meets all the criteria for a modern building material.

Our log houses are ecological, energy-saving, natural and do not pollute the environment. In case of demolition, a log house can be almost completely recycled.

Log houses deliver excellent thermal insulation and create a comfortable living environment — cozy, yet modern. They regulate humidity naturally, offering pleasant coolness in summer and cozy warmth in winter.

  • Natural and eco-friendly material
  • Excellent thermal insulation
  • Natural humidity regulation
  • Full recycling upon demolition
  • EnEV compliant

Solar Thermal

Harnessing Solar Heat

Solar thermal uses the sun's heat via collectors to warm a heat-transfer fluid. The heat is stored and can be used for domestic hot water, heating support, pool heating, or industrial processes — ecological and economical.

  • Domestic hot water
  • Heating support
  • Pool heating
  • Industrial processes
